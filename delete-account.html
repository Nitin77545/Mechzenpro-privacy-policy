Delete Account – MechZenPro

If you want to delete your MechZenPro account and associated data, please follow the steps below.

  1. Send an email to mechzenpro@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request
  3. Mention your registered phone number

Data that will be deleted

  • User account
  • Mechanic profile
  • Service and billing records
  • Location data

Some data may be retained for up to 30 days if required for legal, security, or compliance purposes.

After verification, your account and associated data will be permanently deleted.
