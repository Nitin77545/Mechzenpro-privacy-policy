MechZenPro – Customer Privacy Policy

MechZenPro respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data when you use the MechZenPro – Customer mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Name and mobile number
  • Email address (if provided)
  • Location information to find nearby mechanics
  • Booking and service request details
  • Device information and app usage data

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Connect you with nearby mechanics
  • Process service bookings
  • Send service-related notifications
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Prevent fraud and misuse

3. Location Information

Location data is collected only while the app is in use to show nearby mechanics and improve service accuracy. We do not collect location data when the app is closed.

4. Notifications

We may send notifications related to bookings, service updates, and important app information. Notification permissions can be managed from your device settings.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Data may be shared only:

  • With mechanics to fulfill service requests
  • With trusted service providers required to operate the app
  • When required by law or to protect users and the platform

6. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services such as Google Maps and Firebase to provide location services, analytics, and notifications. These services operate according to their own privacy policies.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access or update your personal information
  • Request deletion of your account
  • Contact us regarding privacy concerns

9. Children’s Privacy

MechZenPro – Customer is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
